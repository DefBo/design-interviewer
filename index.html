Questions

Does this replace our final interview?+

It replaces the need for a design lead to run every one. Most teams still meet shortlisted candidates — the report tells you what to ask them.

What does the candidate need?+

A browser, a microphone and about 25 minutes. No account or app.

Can candidates use ChatGPT during the call?+

Voice makes it awkward, and follow-ups on their own decisions are hard to answer secondhand. But no tool reliably detects outside help, and we don’t claim to.

Can we use our own test task?+

Yes. Attach the brief and the candidate’s solution as a file or a Figma link, plus their note. The interviewer asks about the decisions in it, and the report shows it as a source.

Which roles do you cover?+

Junior, Middle, Senior and Lead Product Designers — the questions and the bar adapt to the level.

What happens to candidate data?+

Only your team sees reports and transcripts. Data is deleted on request or after 30 days.
